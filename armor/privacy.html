Privacy Policy

Bible Learning Adventure is a children’s Bible learning app. This policy is written for parents.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Who this app is for

The app is made for young children with a parent or teacher nearby. It teaches letters, numbers, colors, prayers, and Bible stories.

What we collect

We do not ask children to create an account. We do not ask for names, emails, or phone numbers to use the lessons.

The app may save, on the device only:

  • which letters or lessons have been visited
  • the language the family chose

That information stays on the phone or computer. It is not sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No selling of information
  • No tracking children across other apps or websites
  • No social media logins

Sound and pictures

Lesson pictures and voices are stored with the app’s website files so the lessons can play. They are teaching materials, not recordings of your child.

Hosting

The app is hosted as a website at GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep normal technical logs (such as that a page was opened) as any website host does. We do not use that to identify children.
